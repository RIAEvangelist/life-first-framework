# Contributing

Life First welcomes practical, evidence-seeking contributions that preserve the framework's protective boundaries.

## Before proposing a change

For a substantive proposal, include:

- the problem and affected people;
- the legitimate use case and excluded uses;
- foreseeable misuse and failure modes;
- which safeguard, role, template, or requirement changes;
- measurable benefit and counter-metrics;
- implementation and migration impact; and
- provenance and reuse terms for every non-original element.

Use the [change proposal template](templates/CHANGE_PROPOSAL.md).

## Change classes

- **Editorial:** clarity, spelling, navigation, or formatting without changing meaning.
- **Substantive:** new or changed requirement, role, risk tier, template, metric, or operating guidance.
- **Safeguard-impacting:** any change that weakens, removes, delays, narrows, or creates an exception to a protective control.

Safeguard-impacting changes require explicit rationale, alternatives, migration notes, and independent safety or domain review as described in [GOVERNANCE.md](docs/GOVERNANCE.md).

## Provenance rules

Do not submit private correspondence, credentials, personal data, confidential information, classified material, contraband, or content you are not authorized to share.

For non-original material, identify:

- creator and source;
- license or written permission;
- attribution, redaction, and use conditions; and
- any privacy, confidentiality, classification, or trademark boundary.

A public URL is not itself a reuse license. When terms are absent or unclear, link to the source instead of copying it.

## Pull requests

Keep changes focused. Update the changelog when behavior, requirements, templates, or public claims change. Describe validation performed and any known limitation. Do not claim organization-wide or independent certification.

By contributing original material, you agree that it may be distributed under the repository's MIT License unless the pull request clearly states compatible alternative terms accepted by the maintainers.

## Sensitive safety reports

Do not place secrets, personal data, exploitable operational details, or reports of active harm in a public issue. Follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
