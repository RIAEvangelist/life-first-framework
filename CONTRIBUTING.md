# Contributing

Contributions must have clear provenance and be suitable for public release.

Do not submit private correspondence, credentials, personal data, confidential information, classified material, or content you are not authorized to share. Include attribution and licensing details for all non-original material.

For every non-original contribution, identify:

- the creator and source;
- the applicable license or written permission;
- any attribution, redaction, or use conditions; and
- whether the contribution contains personal, confidential, or restricted information.

A public URL is not itself a reuse license. When reuse terms are unclear, link to the source instead of copying it.
