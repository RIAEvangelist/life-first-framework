# Life First Framework

**Public working draft · v0.3**

Life First is an open, practical framework for consequential AI work. It helps teams protect life and dignity, preserve agency, make proportionate decisions, assign accountable humans, and learn visibly from mistakes.

It is designed for product teams, public-interest technologists, community organizations, safety and trust teams, reviewers, and institutional decision-makers. It is not a certification, legal opinion, emergency protocol, or substitute for domain expertise.

## The operating cycle

Use one auditable cycle for consequential decisions:

1. **Frame** the legitimate purpose, authority, stakes, and affected people.
2. **Verify** facts, sources, uncertainty, and data necessity.
3. **Compare** multiple options, including delay, no action, and non-AI alternatives.
4. **Authorize** the proportionate path with the required accountable review.
5. **Act** only within the approved scope, retention, and escalation boundaries.
6. **Observe** outcomes, errors, near misses, appeals, and human impact.
7. **Repair** harm, correct records, update controls, or retire the practice.

## Use it now

For a new or changed AI workflow:

1. Complete the [system intake](templates/SYSTEM_INTAKE.md).
2. Assign a risk tier using the [decision protocol](docs/DECISION_PROTOCOL.md).
3. Record evidence and uncertainty in the [evidence ledger](templates/EVIDENCE_LEDGER.md).
4. Use the [decision record](templates/DECISION_RECORD.md) before consequential action.
5. Define success and counter-metrics with the [metric card](templates/METRIC_CARD.md).
6. Prepare correction, appeal, rollback, and incident paths before live use.

## Adoption levels

- **Explore:** map one workflow and its human stakes. No claim of conformance.
- **Pilot:** run the bounded pilot with named owners, stop conditions, baseline metrics, and a public-safe learning summary.
- **Operate:** maintain the full control set, review cadence, appeals, incident readiness, change records, and evidence needed to support a scoped conformance statement.

Life First does not authorize a general badge or organization-wide claim. Any conformance statement must name the system, workflow, version, population, risk tier, review date, exceptions, and evidence location.
