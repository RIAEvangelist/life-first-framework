## Agency test

- What can an affected person understand before the consequence?
- Can they refuse or use a human/non-AI alternative without penalty?
- Can they correct inputs and derived records?
- Can they obtain a reason appropriate to the context?
- Can they appeal to a competent person with correction authority?
- Is the path accessible across language, disability, literacy, connectivity, and time constraints?
- What happens to people who do not or cannot engage?
- What prevents retaliation?

## Distribution and displacement

- Who receives the benefit?
- Who bears error, delay, surveillance, documentation, or appeal burden?
- Is burden shifted to workers, families, public institutions, or future repair?
- Which effects are not visible in direct-user metrics?
- What lawful, privacy-preserving disaggregation is necessary?
