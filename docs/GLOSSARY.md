# Glossary

## Normative terms

- **MUST / MUST NOT:** required for a scoped Life First conformance statement.
- **SHOULD / SHOULD NOT:** expected unless a documented, reviewed reason justifies another path.
- **MAY:** permitted but optional.

## Operational terms

- **Abstention:** a system's explicit choice not to produce or act on an answer when authority, evidence, confidence, or safeguards are insufficient.
- **Accountable human:** an identified person with competence, information, authority, time, and responsibility to own, challenge, stop, and answer for a decision.
- **Affected-person representative:** a person empowered to challenge burdens, accessibility failures, exclusion, and non-user effects from the perspective of people who bear the consequence.
- **Appeal:** a request for a consequential outcome to be reviewed by a competent human with authority to correct it.
- **Authority:** the legal, contractual, professional, organizational, or consent-based basis that permits an action within defined limits.
- **Conformance statement:** a scoped, evidence-backed self-attestation about one system, workflow, version, population, and review period. It is not certification.
- **Consequence:** the plausible effect of a decision, including indirect and delayed effects.
- **Control:** a preventive, detective, responsive, or restorative measure intended to reduce a defined risk.
- **Correction:** fixing an inaccurate input, record, inference, or outcome and its reachable downstream copies.
- **Counter-metric:** a measure paired with a target metric to expose harm, displacement, or gaming that apparent improvement might hide.
- **Evidence ledger:** a record separating observed facts, sources, inferences, uncertainty, contradictions, and limitations.
- **Exception:** a time-limited, approved departure from a non-prohibited requirement with compensating controls. Prohibited practices cannot receive exceptions.
- **Human review:** reasoned assessment by a competent person with adequate context, time, independence, and authority; not a rubber stamp.
- **Material change:** a change to model, prompt, tool, data, purpose, authority, action, population, interface, reviewer workflow, or control that could alter risk or outcome.
- **Near miss:** an event that could reasonably have caused harm but did not because of detection, intervention, chance, or limited exposure.
- **Non-user:** a person affected by the system without directly choosing or operating it.
- **Purpose expansion:** use of a system, data, or output for a purpose beyond the reviewed and communicated scope.
- **Repair:** containment, correction, restoration, support, learning, and verified prevention of recurrence.
- **Residual risk:** risk remaining after controls are implemented and tested.
- **Rollback:** tested restoration to a known safer state or process.
- **Shadow mode:** evaluation in the real workflow without operational reliance on the AI output.
- **Stop condition:** a predefined observation that requires pausing or ending a workflow.
- **Uncertainty:** a material limit on knowledge, evidence, model behavior, or predicted consequence that could affect the decision.
