# Life First Framework

Status: public working draft, version 0.1

## Purpose

Life First is a decision framework for people building or operating AI systems where mistakes can affect safety, dignity, liberty, access, or essential services. It is not a substitute for law, professional judgment, emergency services, or domain-specific safety standards.

## Commitments

1. **Life and dignity:** Treat protection of life and respect for human dignity as primary design constraints.
2. **Agency:** Preserve informed human choice and provide meaningful ways to question, correct, or appeal consequential outcomes.
3. **Proportionality:** Match intervention to verified risk. Prefer the least intrusive effective action.
4. **Reversibility:** When facts are incomplete, favor actions that can be safely undone.
5. **Accountability:** Assign responsible humans, record the basis for decisions, and make oversight possible.
6. **Learning:** Measure harms, false positives, missed risks, and human impact; publish failures and corrections when lawful and safe.

## Decision loop

For consequential work, use this loop before acting:

1. **Name the stakes.** Who could benefit, be harmed, be excluded, or lose agency?
2. **Verify the signal.** Separate observed facts from inference, uncertainty, and unsupported narrative.
3. **Minimize exposure.** Collect, retain, and disclose only what is necessary for the legitimate purpose.
4. **Choose the proportionate path.** Prefer reversible, lawful, and narrowly scoped action.
5. **Require accountable review.** Put an identified, qualified human in the loop before irreversible or high-impact action.
6. **Record the basis.** Preserve a concise audit trail: inputs, uncertainty, authority, decision, and outcome.
7. **Learn and repair.** Review results, correct errors, support affected people, and improve the system.

## Non-negotiable safeguards

- No vigilantism, harassment, doxxing, or unauthorized surveillance.
- No bypassing access controls or collecting data merely because it is technically reachable.
- No hidden expansion of purpose, audience, or retention.
- No claim that an AI output is verified evidence without appropriate human and technical validation.
- No irreversible action based solely on an opaque score or uncorroborated model output.
- No concealment of material limitations, conflicts, failures, or uncertainty from responsible decision-makers.

## Implementation check

A Life First implementation should be able to answer:

- What human outcome is this system meant to protect or improve?
- What authority permits the action and what boundaries apply?
- What data is necessary, and when is it deleted?
- Who reviews high-impact decisions and who can stop the system?
- How can an affected person seek explanation, correction, or appeal?
- What is logged, who can inspect it, and how are failures reported?
- What evidence would cause the practice to be changed or retired?

## Contributing

This framework is intentionally small and auditable. Proposed additions should include a concrete use case, foreseeable misuse, measurable success criteria, and clear provenance.
