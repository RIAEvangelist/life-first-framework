## Hybrid collaboration model

The collaboration thread records agreement on a hybrid model:

- Life First remains an independent project with its own repository, contributors, development rhythm, stewardship, and public site.
- Selected attestations, protocols, semantic patterns, or governance constructs may be deliberately proposed or contributed to Civic AI Canon.
- Any such contribution should identify the specific public artifact and carry explicit provenance links in both directions.
- No Canon contribution, acceptance, reciprocal link, endorsement, sponsorship, or affiliation is claimed until the relevant public records exist.
