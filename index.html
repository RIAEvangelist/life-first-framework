Life First Framework

Build systems that keep life first.

A public working space for AI practice centered on human dignity, agency, safety, and accountable stewardship.

First principles

  • Protect life and reduce foreseeable harm.
  • Preserve human agency and meaningful oversight.
  • Make limits, assumptions, and uncertainty legible.
  • Keep consequential decisions auditable and attributable.
  • Prefer reversible, proportional action when consequences are uncertain.

From principle to practice

The initial framework offers a compact decision loop for consequential AI work: identify the human stakes, minimize collection, prefer reversible steps, require accountable human review, document the basis for action, and learn without hiding failures.
